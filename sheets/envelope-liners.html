How to use — no glue stick needed for a test fit; tape works for one-off notes.
On A2 envelopes, a plain rectangle usually reads perfectly once the flap is folded.   For pointed flaps, trace the folded flap on the liner and trim along the trace.   Match liner tone to the card: blush scallops for showers, gold ribbon for weddings.
